On the day

Worth knowing

  • Rehearsal is Saturday at 2:00 PM at the venue. The pool party follows from 4:00 to 9:00 at Restore at Carolina Park in Mt Pleasant — allow about 25 minutes to get there.
  • Setup can begin at 9:00 AM. Anything earlier needs venue approval.
  • Stars Lounge is the getting-ready room until the ceremony, then flips to cocktail hour. Anything personal needs clearing out before 5:00.
  • Straight after the ceremony everyone stays in the Cinema for an all-guest photo. Someone has to announce it, or the room empties.
  • Grooms arrive at 3:30 for the final details walkthrough. Wedding party and photographers at 4:00. Doors open to guests at 4:30.
  • Photography runs 4:00 to 9:30 PM — five contracted hours plus a complimentary half hour at the start. No send-off coverage; the photographers leave at 9:30.
  • All amplified music concludes at 10:30 PM and the bar closes at the same time.
  • Venue closes at midnight. PPHG breakdown concludes by 12:30 AM.
